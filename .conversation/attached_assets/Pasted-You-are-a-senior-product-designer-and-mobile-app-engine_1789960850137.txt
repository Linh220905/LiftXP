You are a senior product designer and mobile app engineer.

Build a polished mobile-first fitness app inspired by the core product loop of
modern gamified workout trackers.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT copy any existing app's name, logo, mascot, illustrations, exact UI,
  text, or visual assets.
- Take inspiration from the product mechanics: workout logging, exercise ranks,
  XP, streaks, quests, achievements, mascot interaction and leaderboards.
- The app should feel like a modern fitness game, not a generic calorie tracker.
- Keep the MVP focused. Do not add unnecessary AI features.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT CONCEPT
==================================================

The app turns gym workouts into a progression game.

Core loop:

Onboarding
→ create fitness profile
→ create/select workout
→ perform workout
→ log sets/reps/weight
→ finish workout
→ calculate progression
→ earn XP
→ potentially rank up
→ maintain streak
→ complete quests
→ compete on leaderboard
→ return for next workout.

Core promise:

"Train. Earn XP. Rank up."

The user should feel that every workout contributes to a persistent
character progression.

==================================================
2. TARGET USER
==================================================

Primary users:

- Beginner gym users
- Intermediate lifters
- People who enjoy competition
- People who like game-like progression
- People who already track workouts manually
- Users motivated by streaks, achievements and rankings

The app should NOT require users to be professional athletes.

A beginner must still feel meaningful progression.

==================================================
3. BRAND / VISUAL DIRECTION
==================================================

Create an original brand identity.

Style:

- Modern
- Premium
- Sporty
- Dark-first
- Clean
- Minimal
- Slightly game-like
- High contrast

fitness app + game + social competition.

Do NOT make it look like a generic bodybuilding app.

==================================================
4. ORIGINAL MASCOT
==================================================

Create one original fitness mascot.

The mascot is a small friendly fitness companion.

Characteristics:

- Cute but not childish
- Athletic
- Expressive
- Simple silhouette
- Easy to animate
- Original design
- Can appear throughout onboarding and the app

The mascot should have different emotional states:

1. Welcome
2. Excited
3. Encouraging
4. Celebrating
5. Impressed
6. Disappointed when a streak is broken
7. Celebrating a PR
8. Celebrating a rank-up

The mascot is NOT the main focus of every screen.

It should act as the user's fitness companion.

Example:

After first workout:

"Let's go! You just earned your first XP."

After PR:

"NEW PR! 🔥"

After rank up:

"You're getting stronger."

After missed streak:

"Don't worry. Let's start again."

==================================================
5. ONBOARDING
==================================================

The onboarding should follow the general product philosophy of a
gamified fitness app:

First sell the value.
Then personalize the experience.
Then introduce the mascot.
Then create the user's starting profile.
Then get the user into their first workout quickly.

Target onboarding length:
8–12 screens.

Do NOT make onboarding unnecessarily long.

↓
LOG SETS
↓
FINISH
↓
GET XP
↓
GET RANK / PR
↓
SEE PROGRESS
↓

- mascot reactions
- clear typography

- strong hierarchy
